# Product Requirements Document: India GCC Ecosystem Growth Trends (2023–2025)

## 1. Objective
To measure the macro-level expansion of India's GCC ecosystem from 2023 to 2025 across key dimensions such as workforce, account count, and center density.

## 2. Scope
This analysis focuses on aggregate data for all "GCC-type" centers (as defined in the Entry Timeline PRD) to derive ecosystem-wide growth metrics.

## 3. Key Growth Indicators
The following five dimensions will be measured:

### 3.1 Expansion in GCC Workforce
- **Definition:** The overall percentage growth in the total GCC workforce from 2023 to 2025.
- **Formula:** `((Total Headcount 2025 - Total Headcount 2023) / Total Headcount 2023) * 100`

### 3.2 Net Workforce Addition
- **Definition:** The proportion of the total three-year workforce growth that occurred specifically in the most recent year (2024 to 2025).
- **Goal:** To understand if workforce momentum is accelerating or decelerating in the final year.

### 3.3 Increase in Total GCC Accounts
- **Definition:** The percentage growth in the number of unique parent companies (Accounts) operating GCC-type centers.
- **Timeline:** Growth from 2023 to 2025.

### 3.4 Growth in Total Centers
- **Definition:** The percentage increase in the absolute number of GCC-type centers established.
- **Timeline:** Growth from 2023 to 2025.

### 3.5 Increase in Average Workforce per Center
- **Definition:** Evaluates if centers are scaling up in size.
- **Metric:** Compare average employees per center in 2025 vs. 2023.
- **Formula:** `((Avg Size 2025 - Avg Size 2023) / Avg Size 2023) * 100`

## 4. Data Requirements
- Total Headcount (2023, 2024, 2025)
- Total Unique Accounts (2023, 2024, 2025)
- Total Count of GCC Centers (2023, 2024, 2025)
