# Product Requirements Document: Regional Growth Analysis (2023–2025)

## 1. Objective
To examine GCC growth patterns based on the headquarters location (Parent Region) of the investing companies, revealing which global regions are driving expansion in India.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all GCC accounts, grouped by their global headquarters' region (e.g., North America, Europe, APAC, MENA).

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Regional Grouping
Companies are grouped by the region of their global headquarters. Common regions include:
- North America (US, Canada)
- Europe (UK, Germany, France, etc.)
- APAC (Japan, Singapore, Australia, etc.)
- Others (MENA, LATAM, etc.)

### 3.2 Cumulative Tracking Logic
Similar to the Industry Analysis, we track **cumulative** account counts for each region across three milestones:
1.  **Through 2023**
2.  **Through 2024**
3.  **Through 2025**

**Rule:** Cumulative totals include all accounts established up to that point.
*Example:*
- **Region: APAC**
    - Base (Through 2023): 205 accounts
    - New in 2024: +5
    - **Through 2024 Total:** 210
    - New in 2025: +7
    - **Through 2025 Total:** 217

### 3.3 Analysis Goals
- Identify the dominant source regions for GCCs.
- Detect shifts in investment patterns (e.g., "Is Europe growing faster than North America in the last 2 years?").
- Calculate the regional share of the total ecosystem over the 3-year period.

## 4. Expected Outputs
- Regional Growth Trend Data (2023 vs 2024 vs 2025).
- Comparative growth rates between regions.
