# Product Requirements Document: Company GCC Entry Timeline

## 1. Objective
To establish a definitive timeline for when a company first entered India's Global Capability Center (GCC) ecosystem by identifying the establishment date of their earliest GCC-type center.

## 2. Scope
This logic applies to all companies operating centers in India. It requires analyzing all centers associated with a company to determine the "Entry Year."

## 3. Methodology & Logic

### 3.1 Center Filtering
We must analyze all centers operated by a company and filter for specific GCC-type establishments. Qualifying center types include:
- GCC/GIC (Global In-house Center)
- Shared Service Centers (SSC)
- Centers of Excellence (CoE)
- Global Business Services (GBS)
- R&D (Research & Development)
- Engineering & Design
- IT Centers

### 3.2 Determination of Entry Year
The **Entry Year** is defined as the earliest establishment date found among all qualifying centers for a given company.

### 3.3 Categorization
Once the Entry Year is determined, the company is categorized into one of the following timeline brackets:

| Category | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **2023 & Before** | Earliest GCC-type center established in 2023 or earlier. |
| **2024** | Earliest GCC-type center established in 2024. |
| **2025** | Earliest GCC-type center established in 2025. |
| **Upcoming** | Companies with planned centers that are not yet operational. |

## 4. Example Scenario
**Company:** 3M
- **Total Centers in India:** 8
- **Analysis:**
    - Center A (Sales Office) - Discarded
    - Center B (Manufacturing) - Discarded
    - Center C (R&D Facility) - Established **2011** (Qualifies as GCC-type)
- **Result:**
    - Entry Year: **2011**
    - Category: **2023 & Before**
