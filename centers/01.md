Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Year-Wise GCC Center Growth & Expansion Trends (2023–2025)
1. Objective

To measure and analyze the year-wise growth, expansion patterns, and structural evolution of GCC centers in India from 2023 to 2025 using the Centers table as the primary data source.

This PRD focuses purely on center-level metrics, independent of workforce or account-level deep dives.

2. Scope

This analysis includes:

All GCC-type centers (as defined in Entry Timeline PRD)

Active centers during 2023–2025

Year-wise comparison of center counts and structural attributes

This excludes:

Account-level financial metrics

Individual employee-level analysis

3. Key Center Growth Use Cases
Use Case 1: Year-Wise Center Growth
3.1 Total Centers per Year

Definition:
Total number of unique GCC centers active in each year (2023, 2024, 2025).

Purpose:
To measure ecosystem expansion in terms of physical presence.

Metric Output:

Total Centers – 2023

Total Centers – 2024

Total Centers – 2025

3.2 Year-over-Year (YoY) Center Growth

Definition:
Percentage growth in total centers from one year to the next.

Formula:

For 2024 growth:

((Centers 2024 - Centers 2023) / Centers 2023) * 100


For 2025 growth:

((Centers 2025 - Centers 2024) / Centers 2024) * 100


Goal:
To determine whether center expansion is accelerating or slowing.

3.3 Net Center Addition (Absolute Growth)

Definition:
Number of new centers added year-wise.

Formula:

Centers 2024 - Centers 2023
Centers 2025 - Centers 2024


Insight:
Helps track raw ecosystem expansion momentum.

3.4 Three-Year Center Growth Rate (2023–2025)

Definition:
Overall percentage growth across the full three-year period.

Formula:

((Centers 2025 - Centers 2023) / Centers 2023) * 100


Goal:
Macro-level ecosystem growth measurement.

3.5 Share of Total Growth Occurring in Final Year

Definition:
Proportion of 3-year growth that occurred between 2024–2025.

Formula:

(Net Addition 2025 / Total 3-Year Growth) * 100


Purpose:
To assess whether expansion momentum is recent or earlier.

3.6 Average Centers per Account (Optional Advanced Use Case)

Definition:
Measures whether accounts are expanding into multi-center setups.

Formula:

Total Centers / Total Unique Accounts


Compare:

2023 vs 2025

Insight:
Shows ecosystem maturity and multi-location scaling behavior.

4. Data Requirements (From Centers Table)

To support the above use cases, the following fields are required:

account_global_legal_name
center_status (active/inactive if applicable)
year (snapshot year if using annualized dataset)
