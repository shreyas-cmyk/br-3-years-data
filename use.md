# Use Case Documentation

## Use Case 1: Determining Company GCC Entry Timeline

This use case establishes when a company first entered India's GCC ecosystem by identifying their earliest GCC-type center.

**Methodology:**
We analyze all centers operated by a company in India and filter for GCC-type establishments, which include: GCC/GIC, Shared Service Centers (SSC), Centers of Excellence (CoE), Global Business Services (GBS), R&D, Engineering & Design, and IT centers.

The entry year is determined by the earliest establishment date among these qualifying centers. Once identified, this year is categorized into one of four timeline brackets:
- **2023 & Before**: Companies that established their first GCC-type center in 2023 or earlier
- **2024**: Companies that entered in 2024
- **2025**: Companies that entered in 2025
- **Upcoming**: Companies with planned but not yet operational centers

**Example:**
3M operates eight centers in India. Among these, only certain centers qualify as GCC-type establishments. The earliest qualifying center was an R&D facility established in 2011. Therefore, 3M's GCC entry year is marked as 2011, placing them in the "2023 & Before" category.

## Use Case 2: India GCC Ecosystem Growth Trends (2023–2025)

This analysis measures the expansion of India's GCC ecosystem across five key dimensions, focusing specifically on GCC-type centers.

**Growth Indicators:**

1. **Expansion in GCC Workforce**: Measures the overall percentage growth in workforce from 2023 to 2025 by comparing the change in total headcount against the 2023 baseline.

2. **Net Workforce Addition**: Calculates what proportion of the total three-year workforce growth occurred specifically in the most recent year (2024 to 2025).

3. **Increase in Total GCC Accounts**: Tracks the percentage growth in the number of companies operating GCC-type centers from 2023 to 2025.

4. **Growth in Total Centers**: Measures the percentage increase in the total number of GCC-type centers established during this period.

5. **Increase in Average Workforce per Center**: Evaluates whether centers are scaling in size by comparing the average employees per center in 2025 versus 2023, expressed as a percentage change.

## Use Case 3: Industry-Wise Growth Analysis (2023–2025)

This analysis tracks how different industries have expanded their GCC presence in India over a three-year period.

**Methodology:**
Companies are categorized by their primary industry classification. We then track the cumulative count of accounts across three time periods: through 2023, through 2024, and through 2025 (including upcoming centers).

The cumulative totals build progressively—each year's count includes all previous accounts plus new additions for that year. For instance, if Aerospace & Defense had 24 accounts through 2023, added 1 in 2024, and 2 in 2025, the cumulative counts would be 24, 25, and 27 respectively.

Industries are ranked by total account count, with smaller categories consolidated into an "Others" group to maintain clarity. Finally, we calculate each industry's share of the total GCC ecosystem for each time period, showing how the industry mix has evolved.

## Use Case 4: Regional Growth Analysis (2023–2025)

This analysis examines GCC growth patterns based on the headquarters location of parent companies.

**Methodology:**
Companies are grouped by their headquarters region (such as North America, Europe, APAC, etc.). Similar to the industry analysis, we track cumulative account counts across the three time periods: through 2023, through 2024, and through 2025.

Each region's cumulative total represents all accounts from that region established up to that point in time. For example, if APAC-headquartered companies had 205 GCC accounts through 2023, added 5 in 2024, and 7 in 2025, their cumulative progression would be 205, 210, and 217 accounts respectively.

This reveals which global regions are driving GCC expansion in India and how regional investment patterns have shifted over the three-year period.
